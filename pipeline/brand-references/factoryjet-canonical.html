Common questions

Web design for Austin businesses — answered.

How can you deliver a website in 7 days?
We use an AI-assisted build workflow that compresses the design and development cycle without compressing quality. Our team has built 500+ websites — we know the patterns, the edge cases, and how to move fast without breaking things. 7 days is the standard. Some projects ship sooner. The 5-step process is: discovery call → design brief → build → review → launch.
How does pricing work?
Pricing is on the first call. No discovery fees, no "it depends" quotes that arrive three weeks later. We tell you a fixed price in our first conversation. Most Austin business websites fall in the $599–$2,999 range, depending on scope and integrations. E-commerce and custom AI features are scoped separately.
Do you work with Austin businesses specifically, or is this generic?
We serve SMBs across the US, UK, UAE, and India — but every website is built for the specific market it targets. For Austin clients, that means tech-savvy audiences, high competitor density from South Congress to Domain Northside, and a buyer culture that has no patience for slow-loading pages or websites that look like they were built in 2018.
What technology stack do you use?
Primary stack: Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, deployed on Cloudflare Pages. For e-commerce: Shopify or WooCommerce depending on requirements. For CMS: Sanity or Next.js MDX. We choose the stack that fits your budget, content needs, and the team who will maintain it after launch.
Will my website rank on Google?
Technical SEO is built in on every project: proper heading hierarchy, meta tags, schema markup, sitemap, robots.txt, Core Web Vitals. We also add FAQPage and LocalBusiness schema so AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews can cite your content. Ranking depends on content and competition — we handle the technical foundation.
What happens if you miss the 7-day deadline?
You don't pay. We've held this policy since we started, which is why our team takes scoping seriously. On the rare occasion a delay is caused by something outside both parties' control (e.g., waiting on content from the client), we discuss an adjusted timeline upfront. We've delivered on time on 97% of all projects.
Do I own the website after delivery?
Yes, completely. You own the code, design, and content. We transfer all assets, the GitHub repository, and hosting credentials at launch. There are no ongoing platform fees unless you choose a CMS with its own pricing (like Sanity). We can host it on Cloudflare Pages for free, or hand it to your preferred host.

Book a free 30-minute discovery call using the link below. We'll discuss your business, the website you need, and give you a fixed price on the call. No sales tactics. If we're a fit, we'll kick off immediately. If not, you'll at least know exactly what a project like yours should cost.
