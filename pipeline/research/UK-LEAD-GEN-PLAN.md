## 1. The UK situation — five hard facts

1. **The UK is over-built, not under-built.** We already run ~21 cities × 6 services of dynamic
   pages, 6 bespoke city hubs, 4 national service pages (`/uk/web-design`, `/uk/shopify-development`,
   `/uk/shopify-seo`, `/uk/ecommerce-seo`), the `/uk` hub, and **~38 UK-focused blog posts.**
   The `/uk/[city]/[service]/[platform]` layer (~450 URLs) was already deleted (May 2026) for being
   doorway spam that earned 1 click in 90 days.

2. **Near-zero UK clicks despite big impressions.** GSC UK last 90 days: single-digit clicks total.
   `/blog/best-ai-seo-agencies-uk` = 8,153 impressions at **pos 40.9**; `/blog/best-seo-agencies-uk`
   = 5,433 impressions at **pos 48.6**. Programmatic city pages (Brighton/Bristol/Cambridge/Cardiff/
   Derby…) rank pos 25-70 with **0 clicks**. This is an impression mirage — the same pattern
   `TOPIC-STRATEGY.md` flagged.

3. **Authority is the binding constraint.** Backlink profile (DataForSEO):

   | Domain | DFS rank | Referring domains |
   |---|---:|---:|
   | **factoryjet.com** | **84** | **44** |
   | found.co.uk (SEO) | 273 | 1,044 |
   | propeller.co.uk | 440 | 2,015 |
   | charle.co.uk (Shopify) | 333 | 778 |
   | chilliapple.co.uk | 341 | 452 |
   | harrisdigitalwebdesign.co.uk | 184 | 60 |

   We're 10-45× weaker on referring domains than established UK agencies. Thin pages can't rank
   because the domain has no authority to lift them, not because the content is missing.

4. **Head "[service] agency" SERPs are owned by directories.** For "web design agency uk",
   "seo agency uk", "ecommerce development agency uk" the top results are Clutch, DesignRush,
   Sortlist, GoodFirms, Semrush Agencies, Yell + a few incumbents. Winning those head terms head-on
   is not realistic short-term; **getting listed inside those directories captures the same demand.**

5. **Cannibalization is rampant.** ~9 near-duplicate "UK web design agency" blogs, 4 "choose web
   design agency UK", 5 "online store UK" posts, 2 identical "how to build online store uk smb guide".
   These split authority and are part of why nothing reaches top-10.

**Doctrine for the UK:** do **not** publish more thin pages. (a) Consolidate the duplicate blog
clusters into definitive pillars, (b) build a small set of strong **national service hub pages** for
the winnable commercial terms, (c) fix CTR on pages already ranking pos 7-12, (d) attack **authority**
(directories + digital PR), and (e) run a GEO/AI-citation content layer for the emerging categories
(Agentic/AI commerce) where nobody owns the answer yet.

  UK trade press). Target: close the 44 → 400+ referring-domains gap.

4. **Ongoing:** Tier 3 GEO/blog cadence (1-2/week, interlinked) + authority/PR.

Liverpool, Sheffield) with real local proof + internal links instead.
