# US market price ranges for FactoryJet cost FAQs (fetched 2026-09-30)

Purpose: market ranges to quote in cost FAQ answers. FactoryJet never shows its own prices here.
Every row below was fetched on 2026-09-30. Nothing in the range column was typed from memory.

## How this was gathered, and what we could not see

- Fetch method: `curl --max-time 30` with a desktop browser user agent, then the claim was read in the extracted page text. Page modified dates come from each page's own `dateModified` schema where it had one.
- Clutch returns 403 to curl. Clutch rows were read through the WebFetch tool instead, asking for verbatim text. Those quotes passed through a summarising model, so spot-check them in a browser before publishing one word for word.
- WebFX started returning 429 (Retry-After 3352 seconds) partway through. Two WebFX pages (healthcare SEO, small business website cost) were read from Wayback Machine snapshots instead. The snapshot dates are in the notes. The live URL is what goes in the table.
- This machine resolves to India. shopify.com/pricing served INR prices (Basic ₹1,499/mo yearly), so the USD plan fees come from Shopify's own US blog post, not the pricing page. TikTok's seller pages (seller-us.tiktok.com, shop.tiktok.com) would not connect at all (curl status 000 on 5 URLs, WebFetch SSL close). TikTok fee figures are therefore UNVERIFIED today.
- Upwork returned 403 to curl and WebFetch on all 3 rate pages tried. No Upwork figure is used.
- Clutch figures are averages across all companies listed on Clutch, most of them offshore. Where Clutch shows a US row, that row is used. Treat Clutch "average project cost" as skewed by large projects.
- Em dashes inside source quotes are written as " to " so this file stays dash-free. No other wording in any quote was changed. Table pipes inside quotes are escaped as `\|`.
- Quotes are trimmed to the sentence or table cell that carries the number.

Status key: **VERIFIED** = figure read on the live page (or the named archive snapshot) on 2026-09-30. **SITE-ESTIMATE** = a figure already on factoryjet.com with no fetchable source behind it, or one we could not match to its cited source. **UNVERIFIED** = no source could be fetched or none publishes the figure; no number is offered as fact.

## Conflicts and caveats worth knowing before quoting

- **Shopify Plus build, same vendor, two numbers.** CartCoders' general guide says $40,000 to $120,000+. Its Shopify Plus hiring guide (https://cartcoders.com/blog/shopify-plus/how-much-does-it-cost-to-hire-a-shopify-plus-developer/) says "A full Shopify Plus store build usually costs between $15,000 and $45,000". Quote the range with the source name, or quote both.
- **SEO averages differ by survey.** Ahrefs (439 providers): $2,917 a month average, local $1,557, hourly $111. Backlinko (300+ professionals, updated 29 Dec 2025): average $1,000 to $2,500 a month, established small business $501 to $1,000. WebFX: small business $1,500 to $3,500. Clutch SEO (updated 21 Sep 2026, via WebFetch): average monthly $3,199.19, US hourly "$100 - $149". Pick one source per answer and name it.
- **Clutch "US" rows can look low.** Clutch lists app development at "United States of America $25 - $49" an hour, the same band as its global figure. We report it as published; it does not look like a US agency rate and should not be quoted as one.
- **AI chatbot vs AI integration overlap.** Ripen Apps (https://ripenapps.com/blog/ai-integration-cost/, modified 18 Aug 2026) prices an AI chatbot feature inside a SaaS product at "$15,000–$60,000" and says AI integration "can range from $15,000 for basic AI features to over $500,000". That is product-company pricing, higher than McKelvey's small business install range in row 6.
- **Vendor sources.** Rows 1 to 8, 13 to 15, 27, 30 and 34 come from agencies or marketplaces that sell the service. They publish ranges to win work. That is normal for this market, and it is why every FAQ sentence below names the source.
- **GEO mid-market alternative.** The Digital Elevator (https://thedigitalelevator.com/blog/aeo-and-geo-pricing-guide/, modified 23 Jul 2026) puts mid-market AEO/GEO retainers at "$2,000 – $8,000/mo", lower than WebFX's $5,000 to $25,000+.
