## Market reality

The raw India file holds **1,236,720 monthly searches** across the seven lines. After stripping career, course, salary, "how to become", exam-definition, DIY-template, ad-blocking and B2C-shopper queries, **about 510,600 is left that a business owner could plausibly be searching (59% discarded)**. India is the most contaminated market we have measured. The contamination is not evenly spread and it changes which lines deserve blogs.

Blog-shaped volume per line, raw then buyer-usable:

| Service line | Raw | Buyer-usable | Discarded | What got thrown out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Digital Marketing | 430,290 | 206,030 | 52% | 181,250 on "what is digital marketing" variants (PAA is literally "Can I earn 10 lakh per month from digital marketing?"), 7,200 on institute queries, 6,920 on "how to block Google ads", 2,990 on "how to earn money from Google Ads" |
| SEO | 258,460 | 67,910 | 74% | 96,040 on "what is seo" (PAA: "What is a SEO salary?", "Is SEO an IT skill?"), 74,360 on "seo tips" and beginner guides, 19,800 explicit course and beginner terms |
| Web Design | 217,020 | 68,140 | 69% | 73,020 on free templates, 49,500 on Awwwards or Dribbble style inspiration, 21,880 on "what is web design" (PAA: "Do I need a degree to be a web designer?") |
| Marketplace (Amazon) | 148,630 | 104,960 | 29% | 40,550 on "best seller product on Amazon" shopper queries |
| AI Agents & Automation | 88,100 | 39,750 | 55% | 36,410 on definition queries mostly asked by learners, 8,160 on "build an agent from scratch" developer queries, 2,150 on tutorials and college project ideas |
| Ecommerce Development | 86,770 | 21,590 | 75% | 39,440 on B2C shopping ("trends online store" is Reliance Trends, "best online store for sarees"), 22,110 on free templates |
| AI SEO / GEO | 7,450 | 2,240 | 70% | 2,570 on "is 2027 next year ai overview" (an AI-forecast query, nothing to do with search), 1,420 on turning AI Overviews off |

**Strong lines:** Marketplace (Amazon) is the cleanest demand pool in India, 71% usable, with real seller intent and India-specific tax questions no global page answers. Digital Marketing and SEO both carry very large agency-selection and cost clusters that we have never written for India. Web Design has a solid national and tier-2 city agency cluster once the template noise is removed.

**Weak lines, said plainly:**
- **AI SEO / GEO is effectively dead in India.** 7,450 total, and after noise, 2,240. The largest single term is people trying to switch AI Overviews off. On top of that we already published eight GEO and AI-search posts. Do not staff this line for India traffic. Two of the three picks below are deliberately small; the third is a citation asset, not a traffic bet.
- **Ecommerce Development is thin, 21,590 usable,** and the biggest chunk of what survives is small cost and how-to clusters in the 200 to 500 range. Worth three posts, not a programme.
- **AI Agents looks huge and mostly is not.** "what is agentic ai" at 18,100 is an IBM, Coursera and GeeksforGeeks SERP with "What are the 7 types of AI?" in the PAA, which is exam revision. The buyer slice is the "how is this different from the automation I already run" cluster and the "show me real examples" cluster. We already own the vs-comparison slugs, so those are off the table anyway.

**One methodology note.** Volumes in this file cluster into near-identical variants (five phrasings of "best seo company india" at 3,600 each). Where I sum those, the true incremental traffic is far below the sum, because one page ranks for the whole cluster. Treat "total addressable" as cluster size, not additive traffic.
