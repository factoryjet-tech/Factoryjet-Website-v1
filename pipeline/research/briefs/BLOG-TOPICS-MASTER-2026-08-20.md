# Blog Topic Master List, 105 Topics Across 5 Markets

Compiled 2026-08-20. Source: DataForSEO Labs keyword_suggestions (32,478 blog-shaped keywords
across 7 service lines x 5 markets) plus 417 live Google SERP pulls capturing People Also Ask,
AI Overview presence and incumbent domains.

Per-market detail with PAA questions, incumbents and duplication checks lives in
`BLOG-TOPICS-{US,UK,INDIA,UAE,AUSTRALIA}-2026-08-20.md`.

Keyword difficulty is ignored throughout, per instruction. All volumes are real monthly
Google search volume for the named country. Nothing is estimated.

## Read this before commissioning anything

**1. Raw volume in this dataset is roughly 2x reality.** DataForSEO returns one query as many
phrasings that all carry an identical volume and CPC. "seo tips", "seo optimisation tips",
"tips seo" all report 74,000 and they are one query. Every number below counts each cluster once.
Australia is the extreme case: the SEO line reports 1,042,930/mo, of which 1,005,060 is five
phrasings of one 201,000 cluster.

**2. We rank for nothing.** `we_rank` is false on all 417 SERPs sampled, in every market.

**3. AI Overviews fire on 350 of 417 informational queries (84%).** On the SEO, AI Agents,
Digital Marketing and Marketplace lines it is effectively every commercial query. Every post
below has to be built as an extraction target first: answer-first block under the hero, one
claim per paragraph, real numbers with sources. Ranking blue-link-first is planning for a SERP
that no longer exists on these terms.

**4. Landing page coverage is wildly uneven.** US 228 pages, India ~55, UK 20, UAE 2, Australia 1.
Blog traffic converts into a service page or it converts into nothing.
