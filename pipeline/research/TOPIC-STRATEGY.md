## 1. The strategic situation — three hard facts

1. **No page-1 rankings for commercial volume.** DataForSEO's index finds only **79 keywords** where factoryjet.com ranks at all (70 US / 4 IN / 5 UK) and **zero in the top 10**. GSC confirms: 143K US impressions → 65 clicks (0.045% CTR, avg pos 22.6). The domain is too young to win fat-head terms in the short term.
2. **Brand + blog carry the business.** 56% of query-attributed clicks are navigational (`"factoryjet"` / `"factory jet"`). The blog earns **41% of all clicks** and is the *only* asset ranking pos 7–9. Non-branded discovery is ~56 clicks / 90 days.
3. **US/UK programmatic city pages are an impression mirage.** `/us` = 44% of all impressions, 0.07% CTR (ranks page 2–4). `/uk` = 232 pages → 8 clicks. India, by contrast, has the best CTR of any city set and **no dedicated section at all**.

## 2. The doctrine going forward

- **Win on low-difficulty long-tail** (KD ≤ 25) commercial + content — not fat-head terms.
- **India first** — highest winnability (many KD < 15 terms at real volume), best existing CTR, currently underbuilt.
- **Own the emerging AI categories now** (AEO/GEO, AI-automation-agency, AI-agent-development) while difficulty is still low — this also matches the AI-native positioning.
- **Fix the pages already ranking** before building more (CTR leaks + host consolidation).

## 4. Market plays

**India — the single biggest winnable opportunity (universe cluster score ≈ 407K, hundreds of KD < 25 terms).**
- Build a real `/in` section (currently none). City × service matrix: SEO / web design / ecommerce / digital marketing for Mumbai, Delhi/Noida, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Indore, Bangalore.
- Target `[service] in [city]` + `best [service] in india` + `near me` — many at KD 6–24.
- India already has the best CTR on the site; this is where new content converts fastest.

**US — mid-tail commercial + the AI categories.**
- Service pages for: SMB web design, SMB/local SEO, SEO audit + pricing, ecommerce development, **AI automation agency**, **AI agent development**. Most target KDs are 0–20.
- Fix, don't multiply, the city pages (see §5).

**UK — narrow, opportunistic.**
- Thin winnable set. Best bets: `small business web design` (1,000, KD 5–13), `ecommerce seo/ppc agency` (KD 0–6), `seo audit services` (880, KD 0), `ai automation agency uk` (210, KD 7). Don't over-invest here yet.

## 5. Page-level fixes (from GSC — do these regardless of new content)
- **Consolidate homepage host variants** (`factoryjet.com` / `www.` / `http://`) to one canonical — currently splits 93 + 11 + 1 clicks and dilutes authority.
- **Fix the two big CTR leaks:** `/blog/ai-adoption-us-small-businesses-2026` (31,190 impr, pos 8, 10 clicks — title/snippet rewrite) and `/us/austin/seo` (20,217 impr, pos 23 — thin page, ranks low despite KD 4 on "austin seo agency").
- **Improve or prune the US/UK programmatic city pages** — they rank page 2–4 on low-KD terms, so the ceiling is page quality/authority, not difficulty. Either strengthen (unique local proof, internal links) or noindex the weakest to stop diluting crawl.
- **Ignore the noise:** `"mobile-only design"` definitional queries (0 clicks), the `"ahrefs how long does seo take"` set (0 clicks), and ~53 bot-looking `"29574: seo track"` queries. Not real demand.
