## How much does SEO cost in UK?

SEO cost in UK, anchored to the most detailed published source (Whitehat SEO's 2026 UK pricing guide):

| Tier | Monthly cost | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Local / startup | £500–£1,200 | Local SEO, single-location businesses |
| Growing SME | £1,500–£3,000 | Where "meaningful work" starts (~£1,500/mo) |
| Mid-market / B2B | £2,500–£5,000 | National competition, content programs |
| Enterprise | £5,000–£20,000+ | Multi-market, multi-domain |

One-off work: audits £500–£7,500; migrations £1,000–£15,000. Hourly: £40–£100 freelance, £70–£300 agency. 78% of UK providers bill monthly retainers, and prices are up 15–30% since 2024 — driven largely by AI-search scope (AEO/GEO adds 20–50% to traditional scope per the same guide). If you are comparing quotes, ask explicitly whether AI search is included or an add-on; that single question explains most price gaps.

## How to choose — segment first, then proof

The single most important filter is **segment fit**: an enterprise media shop will under-serve a 10-person SME (you'll get the B-team), and an SME shop will drown inside a multi-market enterprise brief. Decide your segment from the table above before comparing anything else. Then apply two proof tests: ask for **one case study with revenue numbers** (not traffic graphs — revenue), and ask **who, by name, will run your account** and how much senior time you get monthly. Finally, get the **price in writing** before any discovery call eats your calendar; the agencies that resist are telling you something.
