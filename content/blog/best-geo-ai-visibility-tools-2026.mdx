> **Quick answer:** The best GEO tools in 2026 split into three jobs. AI visibility tracking (Otterly.AI, Peec AI, Profound, Scrunch AI), GEO content optimization (Surfer SEO, Frase, Clearscope, MarketMuse), and the technical SEO foundation (Semrush, Ahrefs, Google Search Console). For a small business, pair one visibility tracker with one content tool instead of buying an enterprise suite. Trackers start around 25 dollars per month; enterprise platforms run into the hundreds.

## GEO Tools Do Three Jobs, and You Probably Need Two

A GEO tool either tells you whether AI engines mention you, helps you write content those engines will quote, or maintains the technical SEO foundation both depend on. Buyers get oversold because vendors blur these three jobs into one "AI SEO platform" pitch and charge accordingly.

Here is the honest version. You need a **visibility tracker** so you can see whether ChatGPT and AI Overviews name your business. You need a **content tool** so the pages you publish are structured to be quoted. You almost never need a brand-new technical suite, because if you already run Semrush, Ahrefs, or even just Google Search Console, your foundation is covered. Two purchases, not five.

This matters because AI search has become a real channel, not a science experiment. Google now shows AI Overviews on close to half of all searches, around 48 percent by [BrightEdge](https://www.brightedge.com/) tracking in early 2026, and ChatGPT passed 900 million weekly active users in February 2026 ([OpenAI, via Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/chatgpt-900-million-weekly-active-users-470492)). When a buyer asks an AI assistant "who is the best web designer in Austin," the businesses it names win the lead. The ones it skips never knew the conversation happened.

## How We Test a GEO Tool Before We Recommend It

Before any tool touches a FactoryJet client account, it passes four checks. You can use the same four to filter anything on this page.

First, **does it query live AI engines** rather than guessing from search data? Real visibility tracking sends actual prompts to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google, then reads the answers. Second, **how many surfaces does it cover?** A tracker that only checks Google AI Overviews misses ChatGPT, which is where a huge share of buyer questions now go. Third, **can you track the prompts your buyers actually type**, in your own words, not a generic keyword list? Fourth, **is the price sane for your size?** A sole trader does not need a 332 dollar per month enterprise plan to learn they are invisible in three prompts.

That last point is where most of the budget gets wasted, so the recommendations below lead with price-to-value, not feature count.

## Best AI Visibility Tracking Tools (Are You Showing Up in ChatGPT?)

These tools answer one question: when AI engines respond to your buyers, do they mention you? This is the category nobody had two years ago and almost everyone needs now.

### Otterly.AI, best budget entry

Otterly.AI is the easiest place for a small business to start. Its Lite plan runs about 25 dollars per month and tracks 15 prompts daily across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot, with the Standard plan around 160 dollars per month for 100 prompts ([pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing/)). For a local service business that wants to know whether it appears for ten core questions, this is enough.

### Peec AI, best clean reporting

Peec AI is the European favorite, starting around 89 euros per month for 25 prompts and rising to roughly 199 euros for the Pro plan with 100 prompts and Slack alerts ([pricing](https://peec.ai/)). Reporting is clean and competitor benchmarking is built in, which makes it a good fit when you need to show a client or a boss where you stand against three named rivals.

### Profound, best for serious tracking

Profound is the platform you graduate to when AI visibility becomes a board-level number. The Starter plan is about 82.50 dollars per month billed annually for 50 prompts, and the Growth plan jumps to roughly 332.50 dollars for 100 ([pricing](https://www.tryprofound.com/)). It is overkill for a five-person shop and exactly right for a brand that lives or dies by AI answers.

### Scrunch AI, best for teams and agencies

Scrunch AI starts around 250 dollars per month for its Core plan, covering 125 prompts, four engines, and five user seats ([pricing](https://www.scrunchai.com/)). The seat count is the tell: this is built for teams that manage AI visibility across multiple brands, which is why agencies like ours look at it rather than a solo founder.

### Ahrefs Brand Radar and Semrush, if you want it bundled

If you already pay for Ahrefs or Semrush, check whether AI visibility is included before buying a standalone tracker. Ahrefs has folded AI mention tracking into its suite, and Semrush offers AI search reporting in its higher tiers. Coverage and limits change often, so confirm what your current plan includes rather than assuming. For a business that hates adding another subscription, a bundled feature you already pay for beats a perfect standalone tool you forget to open.

## Best GEO Content Optimization Tools (Write What AI Will Quote)

A tracker tells you that you are invisible. A content tool helps you fix it by structuring pages so AI engines can extract clean answers. AI assistants quote content that is direct, well formatted, and backed by specifics, so these tools earn their keep.

### Frase, best value

Frase is the budget winner at about 15 dollars per month for unlimited content editor access, with a Team plan near 49 dollars, and AI writing included in every plan ([pricing](https://www.frase.io/pricing/)). It is strongest at briefs, outlines, and pulling the questions real searchers ask, which is the raw material of answer-first content.

### Surfer SEO, best all-rounder

Surfer SEO is the tool most teams settle on. Its Essential plan is about 89 dollars per month billed monthly, or 69 dollars annually, with the Scale plan around 129 dollars ([pricing](https://surferseo.com/pricing/)). Surfer scores your draft against what currently ranks and now flags AI-search signals, which makes it a practical bridge between classic SEO and GEO.

### Clearscope, best for accuracy

Clearscope starts around 129 dollars per month for ten reports and sits at the premium end near 170 dollars per user ([pricing](https://www.clearscope.io/pricing)). You pay for precision: its content grading is the cleanest in the category, which is why agencies producing high-stakes pages keep it in the stack.

### MarketMuse, best for topical authority

MarketMuse starts around 149 dollars per month and is built for planning clusters, not just scoring single pages ([pricing](https://www.marketmuse.com/pricing/)). If you are building topical authority the way this very blog cluster does, MarketMuse maps the gaps. For a one-off post, it is more than you need.

## The Technical SEO Tools GEO Still Runs On

GEO does not replace technical SEO, it stands on it. If AI crawlers cannot reach, parse, and trust your pages, no amount of clever formatting gets you cited. Three tools cover the foundation.

**Google Search Console** is free and non-negotiable. It shows the real queries, clicks, and indexing status that every paid tool only estimates. **Semrush** (from roughly 140 dollars per month) and **Ahrefs** (from roughly 129 dollars per month) cover keyword research, backlinks, and site audits, and both now layer AI-search features on top. **Screaming Frog** crawls your site to find the broken links, thin pages, and schema gaps that quietly sink AI visibility, and it is free up to 500 URLs. Most small businesses already own one of these. If you do, your technical job is mostly done.

## Which Tools to Skip in 2026

Three categories waste money, and saying so out loud is the point of this list.

Skip **single-engine trackers.** A tool that only watches Google AI Overviews leaves ChatGPT, the busiest engine for buyer questions, completely dark. Skip **autopilot AI SEO tools** that promise to write and publish ranking content with no human in the loop. They produce exactly the thin, generic pages Google's helpful content systems are built to demote, and AI engines do not quote them either. And skip **free browser extensions** that brand themselves as AI SEO tools but are really a keyword lookup with a new logo. A free tool is only a bargain if it does a job you actually need.

## GEO Readiness Checklist (Score Yourself)

Before you buy anything, run this seven-point check. Five or more "yes" answers means a tracker is your next move. Four or fewer means fix the content first.

1. Have you asked ChatGPT and Perplexity your top five buyer questions and noted whether you appear?
2. Do your key pages open each section with a direct, one-sentence answer?
3. Do you have FAQ content with FAQPage schema on your money pages?
4. Is your site crawlable, fast, and free of broken links?
5. Do you publish original data, prices, or first-hand examples competitors do not?
6. Are you mentioned on third-party sites AI engines trust, such as Reddit, LinkedIn, or industry directories?
7. Can you name your current visibility in at least one AI engine with a number?

## FAQ

### What are GEO tools?

GEO tools track and improve whether AI engines cite your business when they answer buyer questions. They fall into three groups: visibility trackers, content optimizers, and the technical SEO tools that sit underneath both.

### What is the difference between AI SEO tools and GEO tools?

They are mostly the same tools under two labels. AI SEO is the broad category, while GEO (generative engine optimization) specifically targets generative engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude.

### Can SEO be done by AI?

AI can do parts of SEO well, including drafting, clustering, and auditing, but it cannot replace strategy, original data, or first-hand experience. Those are exactly what AI engines reward when they choose what to cite.

### What does SEO mean in AI?

In an AI context, SEO means structuring your content so large language models can find, trust, and quote it, not just so Google ranks a blue link.

### Do I need a GEO tool if I already use Semrush or Ahrefs?

Usually yes. Classic suites tell you how you rank in Google, while a dedicated visibility tracker tells you whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews actually mention you, which is a different question.

### How much do AI visibility tools cost?

AI visibility trackers start around 25 dollars per month with Otterly.AI Lite and rise to 250 dollars per month or more for team plans like Scrunch AI Core. Enterprise platforms such as Profound scale into the hundreds per month.

### What is the best GEO tool for a small business?

For most small businesses, Otterly.AI is the best starting visibility tracker on price, and Frase or Surfer SEO is the best content tool. Pair one of each rather than buying an enterprise suite.

### Can ChatGPT do SEO?

ChatGPT can help with SEO tasks like outlines, FAQs, and schema drafts, but it has no live ranking data and will invent facts. Treat it as a drafting assistant, not a source of truth.

### Are free AI SEO tools any good?

Free AI SEO tools are fine for spot checks and learning, but most cap prompts or engines. Google Search Console, which is free, remains the most valuable free tool because it shows real query and click data.

### How do I check if my business shows up in ChatGPT?

Ask ChatGPT the questions your buyers ask, such as the best service in your city, and see if you are named. A visibility tracker like Otterly.AI or Peec AI automates this across hundreds of prompts.

### What tracks visibility in Google AI Overviews?

Otterly.AI, Peec AI, Profound, and Scrunch AI all monitor Google AI Overviews. Confirm the engine list on the plan you buy, since cheaper tiers sometimes track fewer surfaces.

### Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO sits on top of SEO. The same crawlable, well-structured, authoritative content that ranks in Google is what gets cited by AI engines.

### What is the best AI SEO tool for content writing?

Frase is the best value content tool at 15 dollars per month with AI writing included. Clearscope is the most accurate for content scoring, and MarketMuse is best for planning topical authority.

### Do GEO tools work for local businesses?

Yes. AI engines answer local queries, and competition is thinner locally, so a single visibility tracker checking near me style prompts can reveal quick wins.

### How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO optimizes for a ranked list of links, while GEO optimizes for being quoted inside a generated answer. GEO leans harder on structured answers, schema, original data, and third-party mentions.

### Which AI search engines should I track first?

Start with ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, since they have the largest audiences, then add Perplexity and Gemini as budget allows.

### Can I do GEO without any paid tools?

Partially, yes. You can manually prompt ChatGPT and Perplexity, use Google Search Console, and structure content well. Paid tools mainly save time and track changes across hundreds of prompts.

### How often should I check my AI visibility?

Monthly is enough for most small businesses, and weekly if you are running an active GEO campaign, since AI answers shift as models update.

### Do AI visibility tools track Perplexity and Gemini too?

Most do. Otterly.AI, Peec AI, Profound, and Scrunch AI cover multiple engines, but the exact list varies by plan, so check before buying.

### What is the fastest way to get cited by AI?

Publish answer-first content with original data and clear formatting, earn mentions on third-party sites that AI engines trust including Reddit and LinkedIn, and add FAQ and schema markup.

### Are these tools worth it for a 5-person company?

A single visibility tracker plus one content tool, roughly 40 to 115 dollars per month combined, is worth it for most 5-person companies. A full enterprise suite usually is not.

### What should I avoid when buying a GEO tool?

Avoid trackers that monitor only one engine, autopilot tools that promise to write and rank with no human review, and free extensions that are just rebranded keyword tools.
