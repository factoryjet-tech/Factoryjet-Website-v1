## What Actually Goes Into the Price of a Website in India

Too many agencies quote a number without explaining what's inside it. Here's every cost component, itemized.

### 1. Domain Name — ₹800 to ₹1,500 per year

Your domain (yourbusiness.in or yourbusiness.com) typically costs ₹800–₹1,500 per year through registrars like GoDaddy, Namecheap, BigRock, or Hostinger. A `.in` domain is cheaper (₹500–₹700/yr) but `.com` still carries more trust in the Indian market.

**Watch out:** Introductory prices can be as low as ₹99/year, but renewals jump to ₹700–₹1,500/year. Always check the renewal price, not just the first-year price.

### 2. Web Hosting — ₹2,000 to ₹15,000 per year

Hosting is where your website lives. Pricing tiers:

- **Shared hosting** (Hostinger, Bluehost India): ₹2,000–₹5,000/year — fine for brochure sites
- **VPS hosting** (DigitalOcean, Linode): ₹6,000–₹24,000/year — needed for medium-traffic sites
- **Managed WordPress** (Kinsta, WP Engine): ₹12,000–₹60,000/year — premium performance
- **Edge/CDN hosting** (Cloudflare Pages, Vercel): Free to ₹2,000/month — best performance for modern Next.js sites

For an ecommerce store, never go with cheap shared hosting. A slow checkout kills conversions. Budget at least ₹5,000–₹12,000/year minimum for hosting a store.

### 3. Design — ₹8,000 to ₹80,000

Design is the most variable cost. Options:

- **Pre-built template**: ₹0–₹8,000 (WordPress themes, Shopify themes) — fast but generic
- **Custom UI design (Figma)**: ₹15,000–₹80,000+ — built specifically for your brand
- **Design system for enterprise**: ₹1,00,000+ — full component libraries, brand guidelines

The difference between a ₹5,000 template site and a ₹50,000 custom design is visible in 2 seconds. If you're building a brand, don't cut corners here.

### 4. Development — ₹10,000 to ₹8,00,000

Development translates design into working code. This is where the cost gap is widest:

- **Basic WordPress site**: ₹10,000–₹30,000
- **Custom WordPress with plugins + SEO**: ₹30,000–₹80,000
- **Custom Next.js / React website**: ₹60,000–₹2,50,000
- **Full-stack web app**: ₹2,00,000–₹10,00,000+

Modern agencies like FactoryJet build in Next.js deployed on Cloudflare — delivering significantly faster load speeds than traditional PHP/WordPress setups.

### 5. Content — ₹5,000 to ₹50,000

Every page needs words, images, and sometimes videos. If you don't supply them, someone has to write them. Professional copywriting in India runs ₹1,500–₹5,000 per page. Stock photography from Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, or free options like Unsplash costs ₹0–₹20,000 depending on usage.

Many small businesses underestimate this. A 10-page site needs approximately 5,000–8,000 words of copy. Budget for it.

### 6. Testing & QA — ₹5,000 to ₹20,000

Professional agencies test across browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox), devices (iPhone, Android, tablets), and screen sizes. For ecommerce, this includes payment flow testing with Razorpay and PhonePe sandbox environments, form submission testing, and speed audits. Most freelancers skip this step. Most agencies include it — and it's worth every rupee.

### 7. SEO Setup — ₹5,000 to ₹25,000

Basic on-page SEO (meta titles, descriptions, schema markup, sitemaps, Google Search Console submission) is often included in agency packages. Technical SEO — Core Web Vitals optimization, structured data, page speed tuning — costs extra.

## Hidden Costs Nobody Tells You About

The quote you receive is rarely the total you'll pay. Here are the costs that appear after you've said yes.

### 1. GST at 18%

Web development services in India attract 18% GST. If an agency quotes ₹50,000, you'll pay ₹59,000 with GST. Always ask: "Is this GST-inclusive or exclusive?" If you have a GSTIN, you can claim input tax credit — effectively reducing the real cost. But many small businesses don't realize GST applies until they see the final invoice.

### 2. Annual Domain Renewal Price Jumps

GoDaddy, Hostinger, and BigRock offer first-year discounts that expire. A domain you registered for ₹99 renews at ₹1,299. Hosting that was ₹399/month renews at ₹699–₹999/month. Read renewal pricing before signing up.

### 3. SSL Certificate

Secure sites (HTTPS) require an SSL certificate. Most quality hosts include a free Let's Encrypt SSL, but some charge separately. Paid SSL certificates (for ecommerce, especially with Extended Validation) run ₹3,000–₹15,000/year.

### 4. Speed Optimization

A site built without performance optimization will fail Core Web Vitals — which directly hurts Google rankings. Speed optimization (image compression, caching, CDN setup, lazy loading) costs ₹5,000–₹20,000 if done after launch. FactoryJet builds this in by default.

### 5. Ongoing Maintenance

Websites are not set-and-forget. Annual maintenance costs:
- WordPress plugin updates + security patches: ₹10,000–₹30,000/year
- Content updates (adding pages, editing text, adding products): ₹5,000–₹15,000/year
- Bug fixes, form maintenance, integration updates: ₹5,000–₹10,000/year

**Total realistic annual maintenance budget:** ₹15,000–₹50,000 for a small business site.

### 6. Redesigns Every 2–3 Years

Web design trends move fast. A site built in 2022 looks dated in 2025. Budget for a partial or full redesign every 2–3 years. Cost: ₹30,000–₹1,50,000 depending on scope.

### 7. Third-Party Tool Subscriptions

Live chat (Tidio: ₹700–₹2,500/month), email marketing (Mailmodo: ₹1,500–₹5,000/month), CRM integration (Zoho CRM: ₹600–₹2,800/month), workflow automation via n8n (self-hosted free, cloud ₹1,800/month). These aren't website costs, but they're real costs of running a digital business.
