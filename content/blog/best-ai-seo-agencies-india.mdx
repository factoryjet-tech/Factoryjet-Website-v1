## How to choose — three questions that expose weak agencies

Ask every agency on your shortlist these three questions before signing anything. One: **"Show me AI citations you earned for a client — per engine."** Real answers name ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini separately; weak answers talk about "AI-readiness." Two: **"Who exactly does my work?"** AI SEO is engineering — schema, llms.txt, site structure. If a sales manager presents and an unnamed team executes, quality is a lottery. Three: **"What will my monthly report show?"** The right answer includes citations per engine, AI Overview appearances, and leads — not just keyword positions.

### What is AI SEO in simple words?

Making your website easy for AI tools — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Google AI Overviews — to find, understand, and mention when people ask them questions. Old SEO got you into Google's list of links. AI SEO gets you named inside the AI's answer.
