## How to choose between them

Three filters separate real AI SEO capability from rebadged classic SEO. First, **per-engine reporting**: ask to see a sample monthly report with citation counts for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini separately, because most agencies track only Google AI Overviews. Second, **who does the work**: AI SEO is engineering-heavy (schema, llms.txt, log-file analysis, entity markup), so if a strategist presents and a mystery team executes, quality varies. Third, **contract terms**: AI search changes every quarter, so month-to-month or short-notice terms keep your agency honest, and you should be wary of 12-month lock-ins on a discipline this young.

### What is AI SEO?

Optimizing a website so AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews) cite it when answering buyer questions. It builds on traditional SEO but adds schema-rich answer formatting, structured FAQ blocks, entity optimization, and third-party citation acquisition.

### What is the difference between GEO and AEO?

GEO targets engines that generate answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini). AEO targets any engine returning a direct answer, including Google AI Overviews and featured snippets. The work overlaps almost completely, so judge agencies on the work, not the acronym.
