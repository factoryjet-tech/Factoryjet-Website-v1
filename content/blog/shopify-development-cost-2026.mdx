Most Shopify cost guides give you a range so wide it's useless. "$1,000 to $100,000" tells you nothing about what you actually need to budget.

At FactoryJet, we've built over 500 Shopify stores for US small businesses. Here are the real numbers, organized by what you're actually buying, verified against current 2026 market data.

## The Short Answer: What Shopify Development Actually Costs

Before getting into the breakdown, here's the honest answer most agencies won't give you upfront:

**A DIY store with a free theme costs $39 to $89 per month** (that's $468 to $1,068 in Year 1 all-in, according to [Shopify's own cost guide](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-website-cost)).

**A professionally built custom store for a small US business typically costs $2,000 to $6,000 one-time** plus $39 to $105/month for the platform.

**A complex custom build with integrations runs $6,000 to $20,000.**

**Enterprise and headless builds start at $40,000 and climb past $200,000.**

Everything else is noise. The right number for your business depends on which of four cost buckets apply to you.

## The Four Cost Buckets Every Shopify Store Has

Every Shopify store has four and only four cost components. Most people budget only for the platform fee and get surprised by the other three.

### 1. Platform Fees (Ongoing)

This is what you pay Shopify every month regardless of who builds your store. Current pricing as of June 2026, verified on [Shopify's pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/pricing):

| Plan | Monthly Billing | Annual Billing | Transaction Fee (no Shopify Payments) |
|------|----------------|----------------|---------------------------------------|
| Basic | $39/month | $29/month | 2% |
| Grow | $105/month | $79/month | 1% |
| Advanced | $399/month | $299/month | 0.5% |
| Shopify Plus | $2,300/month | Lower on annual contract | 0.15% |

Most new stores start on Basic. The Grow plan makes sense when your monthly revenue is above $5,000 to $10,000 and the reduced transaction fee pays for the plan upgrade.

### 2. Theme Cost (One-Time)

Your theme is a one-time purchase that determines how your store looks and what layout options you have. Three tiers:

- **Free themes:** $0. Professional and functional. Good for validating a product idea, but generic.
- **Shopify Theme Store paid themes:** $0 to $200 one-time, per [Shopify's official pricing](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-website-cost). Independent niche-specific themes run a similar range.
- **Custom-designed theme from scratch:** $3,000 to $15,000. Only justified when your brand has requirements no existing theme can meet, such as complex product configurators or heavily branded visual identity.

### 3. Development and Setup Cost (One-Time)

This is where the range widens and where most guides fail. The breakdown by project type:

**Basic store setup** (install theme, upload products, configure payments and shipping): $500 to $2,000 one-time. A competent developer completes this in 2 to 5 days.

**Standard custom store** (custom homepage layout, product page modifications, brand typography, essential integrations like reviews and email): $2,000 to $6,000.

**Advanced custom store** (custom product configurators, subscription logic, B2B pricing rules, ERP or inventory system integration): $6,000 to $20,000.

**Enterprise or headless build** (custom Next.js frontend, Shopify as backend, custom checkout, performance-optimized architecture): $20,000 to $100,000+.

These ranges are confirmed across both [Ogresto's 2026 cost breakdown](https://ogresto.com/blogs/shopify-development-cost-2026) (updated June 2, 2026) and [CartCoders' March 2026 developer cost analysis](https://cartcoders.com/blog/shopify-development/shopify-developer-cost-hourly-rate-project-pricing/).

### 4. Apps and Ongoing Costs (Monthly)

This is the category that surprises most store owners after launch. Some common apps and what they cost:

| App Category | Examples | Monthly Cost |
|-------------|----------|-------------|
| Product reviews | Judge.me, Okendo | $0 to $199 |
| Email marketing | Klaviyo, Omnisend | $20 to $150 |
| Subscriptions | Recharge, Seal | $99 to $499 |
| Loyalty programs | Smile.io | $49 to $199 |
| Upsells | ReConvert, Zipify | $29 to $99 |

A basic app stack (reviews, email, and upsells) costs $50 to $250 per month. A full app stack for a scaling store can reach $500 to $1,000 per month. Source: [Ogresto's 2026 Shopify cost breakdown](https://ogresto.com/blogs/shopify-development-cost-2026).

Shopify's App Store includes more than 8,000 apps, with many free options for early-stage stores.

## What Shopify Developers Actually Charge

If you're hiring a developer or agency directly, here are current 2026 hourly rates by experience level, sourced from [CartCoders' March 2026 breakdown](https://cartcoders.com/blog/shopify-development/shopify-developer-cost-hourly-rate-project-pricing/):

| Developer Type | Hourly Rate |
|---------------|------------|
| Junior Shopify developer | $25 to $45/hour |
| Mid-level Shopify developer | $45 to $90/hour |
| Senior Shopify developer | $90 to $160+/hour |
| Mid-tier agency | $75 to $140/hour |
| Premium US/EU agency | $120 to $200/hour |

US and Canadian agencies typically bill $120 to $200/hour. UK and Western European firms run $90 to $160/hour. The quality gap between a $45/hour mid-level developer and a $140/hour agency is real: agencies include project management, QA, and structured delivery. For a contained task like a homepage section update, the freelancer makes sense. For a full store build or integration project, the agency model produces more predictable outcomes.

## What Drives Shopify Development Cost Up

**Custom design from scratch:** If you need a completely original UI designed before development begins, add $2,000 to $8,000 for design work alone, on top of the development cost.

**Complex product logic:** Configurable products, bundle builders, and custom variant systems require significant custom Liquid or app development.

**Third-party integrations:** Connecting Shopify to an ERP, warehouse management system, or existing CRM adds $1,000 to $5,000 per integration, and $5,000 to $25,000 for enterprise ERP connections like NetSuite. Source: [CartCoders, March 2026](https://cartcoders.com/blog/shopify-development/shopify-developer-cost-hourly-rate-project-pricing/).

**Custom checkout:** Only available on Shopify Plus. Custom checkout UI extensions add $2,000 to $7,000; Shopify Functions development adds $3,000 to $10,000 on top of the Plus plan cost.

**Shopify Scripts migration:** Shopify Scripts are being deprecated. Brands still using them need to migrate to Shopify Functions, a separate development engagement typically costing $4,000 to $12,000.

---

## What Drives Cost Down

**Having your content ready at kickoff:** Product photography, copy, and brand assets already organized cuts developer setup time by 30 to 50%. That's real money when developers bill hourly.

**Keeping launch scope tight:** Launch with what you need to sell, not everything you want. Post-launch additions are cheaper and less risky than trying to build everything at once.

**Choosing a purpose-built theme for your niche:** A theme built for your product category means fewer custom sections needed, which directly reduces development hours.

**Annual Shopify billing:** Switching from monthly to annual saves $10/month on Basic, $26/month on Grow, and $100/month on Advanced. Over 12 months that's $120 to $1,200 back in your pocket.

## What to Ask Any Shopify Developer Before Hiring

**Do you charge fixed price or hourly?** Hourly with no ceiling is a financial risk. Fixed-price projects give you budget certainty.

**Who owns the code after delivery?** You should own everything. Some agencies retain IP rights or build on proprietary systems that lock you into their platform.

**What's included in post-launch support?** Most agencies offer 30 days of bug fixes. Anything after that should be scoped and priced separately before you sign.

**Can you show me stores you've built in my industry?** A Shopify developer who has built 20 apparel stores understands the conversion patterns for apparel. General portfolio work doesn't tell you that.

**What's your approach to mobile performance?** Over 70% of Shopify traffic is mobile. A developer who can't demonstrate a sub-3-second LCP on mobile isn't optimizing for where your customers actually shop.

## FAQ: Shopify Development Cost in 2026

**How much does a basic Shopify store cost in 2026?**
A functional Shopify store using a free or low-cost theme, set up by yourself, costs $468 to $1,068 in the first year. That includes the Basic plan at $39/month, a domain, and basic apps. Source: [Shopify's official cost guide](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-website-cost), updated February 2026.

**What is the cheapest way to start a Shopify store?**
Use Shopify Basic at $39/month, apply a free theme, and build it yourself using Shopify's no-code editor. You can have a working store up for under $500 in Year 1 if you handle the setup yourself and keep apps minimal.

**Is Shopify more expensive than WooCommerce?**
Shopify has higher monthly platform fees but lower development and hosting costs. WooCommerce is free to install but requires separate hosting ($5 to $200/month), paid plugins, and ongoing maintenance. Total cost of ownership over 3 years is comparable for most store sizes.

**How much does a 5-page Shopify store cost?**
A 5-page Shopify store (homepage, product page, collection page, about, contact) professionally built runs $1,500 to $4,000 for setup and design. FactoryJet delivers these in 7 days.

**What does a custom Shopify theme cost?**
A fully custom Shopify theme built from scratch costs $3,000 to $15,000 one-time. Theme customization of an existing paid theme costs $1,500 to $8,000 depending on scope. Source: [Ogresto, June 2026](https://ogresto.com/blogs/shopify-development-cost-2026).

**How much does Shopify app development cost?**
Building a custom Shopify app runs $5,000 to $35,000+ depending on complexity. Simple checkout extensions cost $2,000 to $7,000. Complex custom apps with their own admin UI and third-party API integrations run $15,000 to $35,000+.

**What does WooCommerce to Shopify migration cost?**
WooCommerce to Shopify migration typically costs $3,000 to $12,000. This includes product data transfer, URL redirects for SEO preservation, customer data migration, and theme build. Source: [CartCoders, March 2026](https://cartcoders.com/blog/shopify-development/shopify-developer-cost-hourly-rate-project-pricing/).

**What does Magento to Shopify migration cost?**
Magento to Shopify migration runs $7,000 to $25,000 due to Magento's complex database structure, custom extensions, and the amount of SEO redirect work required for large catalogs.

**How much is Shopify Plus per month?**
Shopify Plus costs $2,300/month as listed on Shopify's website. Enterprise brands on annual contracts may negotiate different terms. The Plus plan includes features like custom checkout, B2B functionality, advanced automation, and dedicated account management.

**How much does a dedicated Shopify developer cost per month?**
A dedicated Shopify developer working full-time costs $3,000 to $6,000/month for remote talent outside the US and $7,000 to $12,000/month for US or European developers. This model suits growing brands with a continuous stream of development work. Source: [CartCoders, March 2026](https://cartcoders.com/blog/shopify-development/shopify-developer-cost-hourly-rate-project-pricing/).

**Can I build a Shopify store myself?**
Yes. Shopify's no-code editor handles layout, colors, fonts, and basic functionality without any coding. A DIY approach with a paid theme gets most stores to a professional standard. Hire developers when you need custom functionality, complex integrations, or your time as a business owner is worth more than the cost of delegation.

**How long does Shopify development take?**
Basic setup: 3 to 7 days. Standard custom build: 2 to 4 weeks. Advanced custom build: 4 to 8 weeks. Headless or enterprise: 3 to 6 months. FactoryJet delivers up to 5-page Shopify stores in 7 days with a delivery guarantee.

**What's the difference between a Shopify theme and a custom build?**
A Shopify theme is a prebuilt design you apply and customize using Shopify's visual editor. A custom build means a developer writes code to create layouts, sections, or functionality that no existing theme provides. Themes are faster and cheaper. Custom builds are for when a theme genuinely can't do what your product or brand requires.

**What apps does a Shopify store actually need?**
At minimum: a product reviews app (social proof), an email marketing app (recover abandoned carts), and basic SEO optimization. A basic app stack runs $50 to $250/month. Don't over-app at launch. Add tools when revenue justifies the cost.

**Does Shopify charge transaction fees?**
Yes, if you don't use Shopify Payments. Transaction fees are 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, and 0.5% on Advanced. Using Shopify Payments eliminates these fees, but Shopify Payments has its own processing rates (2.9% + 30 cents on Basic, scaling down on higher plans).

**Is Shopify good for B2B?**
Shopify Plus includes native B2B features: custom pricing per customer, net payment terms, wholesale portals, and company account management. Standard Shopify plans can handle basic B2B with apps but hit limitations quickly. Shopify Plus B2B development typically costs $40,000 to $120,000+.

**What does Shopify speed optimization cost?**
A standalone Shopify speed optimization engagement costs $1,000 to $5,000 and focuses on image optimization, script reduction, and Core Web Vitals fixes. Research shows speed improvements can increase conversion rates by 15 to 25%.

**How much does custom checkout on Shopify cost?**
Custom checkout is only available on Shopify Plus. Checkout UI extension development costs $2,000 to $7,000 additional. Full custom checkout logic using Shopify Functions runs $3,000 to $10,000.

**What is headless Shopify and how much does it cost?**
Headless Shopify means building a completely custom frontend (typically in Next.js or React) that connects to Shopify's commerce backend via API. The store looks and performs however you design it. Cost range: $60,000 to $200,000+. It makes sense at $500,000+ annual revenue when performance and design flexibility justify the investment. Source: [CartCoders, March 2026](https://cartcoders.com/blog/shopify-development/shopify-developer-cost-hourly-rate-project-pricing/).

**How do I know if I need Shopify Plus?**
Shopify Plus makes financial sense when your annual revenue is above $1 million, you need custom checkout logic, you're running B2B alongside DTC, or you need multi-store architecture. The $2,300/month plan cost is justified when Plus features generate more revenue or operational savings than the upgrade costs.

**Does Shopify include hosting?**
Yes. All Shopify plans include secure hosting, SSL certificates, and unlimited bandwidth. You don't pay separately for hosting. This is one of the genuine advantages of Shopify over self-hosted platforms like WooCommerce, where hosting costs add $5 to $200+/month depending on traffic.
