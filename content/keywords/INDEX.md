## Content Writing Rules

1. **Primary keyword** must appear in: `<title>`, first `<h1>`, meta description, first 100 words of body copy, and at least one FAQ question.
2. **Secondary keywords** must appear in: at least 2 `<h2>` headings and naturally in body paragraphs.
3. **LSI/FAQ terms** must appear in: FAQ section questions/answers and supporting body paragraphs.
4. **Keyword density:** 1–2% max. Never stuff. Always write for humans first.
5. **City pages:** Replace `[city]` with the actual city name in every keyword and heading.
6. **Anti-AI writing:** Use contractions, real numbers, short sentences, specific examples. Avoid 'leverage', 'robust', 'cutting-edge', 'game-changing'.
7. **E-E-A-T signals:** Cite specific project counts (500+), years in business (25+), delivery SLA (7 days), pricing ($1,999+).
8. **FAQ format:** Minimum 20 FAQs per page. Questions should read like real Google searches, not formal headings.
9. **Schema:** Every page needs FAQ schema (FAQPage), BreadcrumbList, and LocalBusiness or Service schema.
10. **AEO/GEO:** Write 2–3 sentence direct answers after each FAQ question — optimized for featured snippets and AI citation.
