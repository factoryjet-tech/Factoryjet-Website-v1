# PRODUCT.md — FactoryJet US site

**Register: brand.** This is a marketing site for an agency; design IS the product. Pages must look designed, not templated.

## What FactoryJet is
FactoryJet is an **AI-native omnichannel commerce solutions agency** — a consulting, development, implementation, and scale-up partner for **DTC and B2B e-commerce**. It is a services/IT-services company, not a SaaS product. The team designs, builds, implements, and scales unified commerce; the client owns and operates what is built (never "we run your store").

- Platform-agnostic: Shopify, Shopify Plus, BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce (Magento), WooCommerce, headless, and Commerceflo. We recommend the fit.
- AI agents = software we build and implement into the client's stack.
- Commerceflo (lowercase f, Commerceflo.ai) = a separate beta product; on FactoryJet it appears only as one engine option. Conversion seed, never the headline.
- Experience framing = **decade-plus / "10+ years"**, NEVER 25 years.
- Most clients are US-based. DTC + B2B.

## SEO entity
Title/H1/Service-schema entity = **"ecommerce development agency"** (and "B2B ecommerce"). Omnichannel commerce is the wrapper concept/story, not the headline SEO entity (it has low commercial search). Evidence: `research/us-keyword-map-2026-06-26.md`, `research/us-tier1-keywords-2026-06-27.csv`.

## Audience
US small-to-mid business owners and operators, $500K–$5M revenue, DTC brand founders, wholesalers/distributors, B2B brands, and marketing/ecommerce managers. They are evaluating whether to hire an agency. They scan; they want proof and clarity, not buzzwords.

## Primary action
Lead capture: the inline hero form (name + email → /thank-you) and "Talk to the Founder" (Calendly). Every hero should carry the inline lead form. Secondary CTA: "Get a commerce audit" (opens the contact modal).

## Surfaces (page types)
- Homepage `/us` (the flagship, fully designed).
- Solution/concept pages: `/us/omnichannel-commerce`, `/us/b2b-ecommerce`, `/us/ecommerce-consulting`.
- Platform pages: `/us/bigcommerce-development`, `/us/headless-commerce`, `/us/commerceflo`, and `/us/services/{shopify,magento,woocommerce}-development`.
- Supporting service pages (`/us/services/*`), city pages (`/us/{city}/{service}`), vertical SEO pages.

## Hard content rules (non-negotiable)
- **No pricing** on any service page (no $ service prices, no tiers). Scope factors + "scope it on a call" only. Market ranges / revenue thresholds / market stats are OK.
- **No em dashes.** Use commas or restructure.
- **No AI-slop verbs:** seamless, streamline, empower, unlock, elevate, robust, transform, leverage, world-class, supercharge.
- **No geo-cheap framing:** never "cheaper than US/UK agencies", never "60-70% cheaper / 75-85% faster / 55-75% lower cost", no offshore-cost framing. Positioning = efficient / fast / quality.
- **Honesty:** real clients only (Bombay Petals B2B, Belle Maison DTC). Never fabricate metrics. No faked logos/results.
- Never say we "run" or "operate" the client's store.
