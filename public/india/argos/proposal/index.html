01
Section One · Start here

Technical SEO

In plain words: Technical SEO is the plumbing of your website. Before Google can rank you, it has to crawl your pages, understand them, and trust they load fast and clean. If the plumbing leaks, even great products stay invisible — and blogging will never work. You asked to start here, and you're right to. Scope: crawlability · indexation · Core Web Vitals · schema verification · site health.

01

Full Technical Audit & Crawl

We scan every page with pro crawlers (Screaming Frog, Sitebulb) plus a full read of your GSC & GA4 — a complete health report of your store.

02

Core Web Vitals & Speed

We fix how fast your site loads and responds (LCP, INP, CLS). Faster pages mean higher rankings and fewer dropped sales — especially on mobile.

03

Crawlability & Indexation

We make sure Google can reach and list your important pages (robots.txt, XML sitemaps, crawl budget) and stop wasting effort on junk URLs.

04

Duplicate Content Cleanup

Shopify creates many copies of the same page through filters and tags. We point Google to the right one (canonical tags) so your ranking power is not split.

05

Schema Audit & Completion

You have Product & Review schema on a stock theme — we verify it's valid and error-free, then add what's missing (Offer, Breadcrumb, FAQ, Organization) for stars, price & stock in results.

06

Site Architecture & URLs

We organise collections and products in a clean, logical structure with tidy web addresses so shoppers and Google find their way easily.

07

Internal Linking

We connect your pages smartly so ranking strength flows to your best-selling collections (link equity distribution).

08

Shopify Theme / Code Optimisation

We trim heavy code, lazy-load images and remove render-blocking scripts (Liquid, deferred JS/CSS) without breaking your design.

09

Image Optimisation

We compress and convert images (WebP, alt text) so pages load faster and also show up in Google Images.

10

Redirects & Error Fixes

We repair dead links and set proper redirects (301s, 404 cleanup) so no shopper — or Google — hits a wall.

11

Mobile & JavaScript SEO

We confirm your site works perfectly on phones and that Google can read JavaScript-loaded content (mobile-first indexing, render checks).

12

Monitoring & Reporting

Ongoing Search Console monitoring and a clear monthly report of every fix and its impact — so you're never in the dark.
