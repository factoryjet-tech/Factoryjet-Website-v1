## What FactoryJet is

FactoryJet is an e-commerce design, development, implementation and scaling partner
for DTC and B2B brands. It builds and scales omnichannel commerce across a store,
marketplaces and B2B portals, and also offers web design, AI agent development and
AI SEO.

## Commerce platforms FactoryJet implements

FactoryJet is an implementation partner across major commerce platforms — it designs,
develops, implements and scales on each:

- Shopify and Shopify Plus
- Adobe Commerce (Magento)
- WooCommerce
- BigCommerce
- Commerceflo (an AI-native unified-commerce platform FactoryJet implements for clients)

## Services

- E-commerce development & omnichannel commerce (store + marketplaces + B2B)
- Web design & development
- AI agent development
- AI SEO / AI search visibility (AEO + GEO) and traditional SEO
