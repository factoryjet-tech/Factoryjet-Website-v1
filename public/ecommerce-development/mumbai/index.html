Platform & Tech
Shopify or WooCommerce — which is right for my Mumbai business?

Shopify is the right choice for most Mumbai D2C brands — faster to launch, easier to manage, and Razorpay/Shiprocket integrations are rock-solid. WooCommerce suits businesses already on WordPress, those who need deep content-commerce integration, or brands wanting zero platform fees and maximum code flexibility. Magento/Adobe Commerce is for enterprises with 10,000+ SKUs and ERP systems. We'll recommend the right platform in a 15-minute consultation — and build whichever you choose at the same quality and in the same 7-day timeline.

Can you migrate my existing WooCommerce, Magento, or OpenCart store?

Yes — platform migration is one of our highest-demand services. We handle the complete migration: full product catalogue, customer data, order history, and all SEO 301 redirects so you don't lose a single ranking. We've completed 40+ platform migrations for Indian brands with zero ranking drops post-migration. Most WooCommerce-to-Shopify or OpenCart-to-Next.js migrations complete in 7 days. We offer a pre-migration audit — we'll map your current store and show you exactly what moves and what needs rebuilding.

Do you build custom headless ecommerce stores?

Yes — our Custom / AI Commerce tier runs on Next.js 14 (App Router) with Shopify or WooCommerce as the backend, Cloudflare for edge delivery, and Firebase for real-time features. Headless is ideal for brands needing sub-1-second LCP, non-standard checkout flows, or deep ERP/inventory integration. If you have a mobile app alongside your store, headless also gives you a unified API layer. We've built headless stores for fashion, B2B wholesale, and subscription-box models.

Which payment gateways and shipping tools do you integrate?

Payment: Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, Paytm, HDFC PayZapp, CC Avenue, and all UPI methods. COD flows, BNPL through LazyPay and Simpl, EMI through HDFC, ICICI, and SBI cards. Shipping: Shiprocket (integrated with 20+ courier partners), Delhivery, BlueDart, Ecom Express, and Amazon Shipping. WhatsApp order notifications via official Business API. We've integrated every major Indian payment and logistics rail across 500+ projects — on Shopify, WooCommerce, and custom builds.

Will AI features slow down my store?

No — and this is a common misconception. Our AI features run on Cloudflare edge functions and async API calls, so they never block the initial page render. Speed & SEO
Will my store load fast on Indian 4G networks?

4G is the constraint we optimise for first. Every store ships with WebP images, aggressive lazy loading, and Cloudflare edge caching with Indian PoPs in Mumbai, Delhi, and Chennai. Our average delivered LCP is 1.6 seconds on 4G simulation. The Indian ecommerce industry average is 4.8 seconds. Every 1-second improvement in load time increases conversions by 7% — and on mobile 4G, where the majority of Indian shoppers browse, the gap between us and a typical freelancer is costing you real sales.

What Lighthouse score do you guarantee?

Lighthouse 95+ across Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO — on both mobile and desktop. This is a written guarantee in your contract. Core Web Vitals targets: LCP under 2.5 seconds, CLS under 0.1, INP under 200ms. We run automated Lighthouse CI on every deployment so regressions are caught before they reach production. Lighthouse screenshots are included in your handover package — proof you can share with anyone who asks.

What SEO do you include with every store?

Every store includes keyword-optimised page titles and meta descriptions, semantic H1–H3 hierarchy, canonical URLs, XML sitemap auto-generation, robots.txt configuration, JSON-LD structured data (Product, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, LocalBusiness schemas), and Open Graph tags. On Growth and Custom tiers, we add image alt-text optimisation across all products, a post-launch technical SEO audit, and 301 redirect mapping for any URLs being changed. AI SEO — Google AI Overview and ChatGPT citation optimisation — is available as a monthly add-on.

How long before I start getting organic traffic from Google?

New domains are typically indexed within 2–4 weeks and start ranking for lower-competition local terms within 6–12 weeks. For brand-new businesses, terms like "handmade jewellery Mumbai" or "organic cleaning products online" often appear in the top 20 within 4–8 weeks with proper on-page optimisation and a Google Business Profile. Established domains migrating platforms see ranking recovery within 4–6 weeks when 301 redirects are correctly implemented — which is exactly why our migration process is so thorough.
